# Acid Sulfate Soils Management Plan - Ironbark Industrial Estate

**Version:** v1.0 (Final)  
**Date:** 2026-03-20  
**Author:** [[Marcus Oyelaran]]  
**Project:** [[p4-ironbark-gme]]

> FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT. Not a real report.

## 1 Findings

Sulfidic material was found at 2 to 4 m in 22 of 30 boreholes. Groundwater is at about 1.8 m. Dewatering for the drainage upgrade will mobilise acidity and metals and requires treatment before discharge.

## 2 Treatment approach

Dewatering discharge will be treated by lime dosing at 1.5 times the stoichiometric rate to meet a discharge pH of 6.5 to 8.5. (This approach was later reversed; see addendum and the 13 May 2026 decision.)

## 3 Table 4 trigger values

| Parameter | Trigger value |
|---|---|
| pH | 6.5 to 8.5 |
| Dissolved zinc | 0.08 mg/L |
| Dissolved aluminium | 0.5 mg/L |

These are project trigger values for this fictional demonstration.

## 4 Monitoring

Quarterly groundwater monitoring is planned for eight rounds at six monitoring wells (MW01-MW06). The contract was later extended to ten rounds.
