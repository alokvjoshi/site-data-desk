# Remedial Action Plan - Halden Road Former Fuel Depot

**Version:** v1.0 (Final)  
**Date:** 2026-04-02  
**Author:** [[Imogen Hartley]]  
**Project:** [[p1-halden-road]]

> FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT. Not a real report.

## 1 Remediation objective

The objective is to remove the lead-impacted fill at TP14 and the hydrocarbon-impacted soil at BH07 so that the site is suitable for commercial/industrial use, validated against the same criteria used in the DSI.

## 2 Options considered

| Option | Description | Programme | Indicative cost impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Excavate to 3.5 m and dispose off-site | 8 weeks | baseline |
| B | Ex-situ bioremediation of hydrocarbon soil, off-site disposal of lead fill | 18 weeks | about A$140k lower for hydrocarbon soil |

Option A was selected because it is faster and gives more certain validation.

## 3 Validation plan

Validation comprises 18 samples from excavation bases and walls, analysed for TRH, BTEXN and lead, plus re-sampling of two monitoring wells after backfill. Results are compared with the DSI criteria. Any exceedance requires further excavation or a documented risk-based justification.

## 4 Programme and approvals

Excavation is scheduled to start 18 May 2026 and validation to complete on 28 August 2026. The closure report is due 13 November 2026 and will be submitted to the site auditor. Dewatering controls are required below 2.5 m in the north-east pit.
