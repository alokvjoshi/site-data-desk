# Detailed Site Investigation - Halden Road Former Fuel Depot

**Version:** v1.0 (Final)

**Author:** [[Imogen Hartley]]  
**Project:** [[p1-halden-road]]

> FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT. Not a real report.

## 1 Introduction

This report presents the detailed site investigation (DSI) of the former Halden Road fuel depot for Halden Fuels Pty Ltd (fictional). The site is zoned commercial/industrial and the assessment therefore adopts the commercial/industrial land use scenario (HIL D) from NEPM ASC Schedule B1. The objectives were to characterise soil and groundwater impacts around the former tank farm and the north-east filled area and to recommend whether remediation is needed.

## 2 Investigation program

The field program comprised 26 boreholes, 6 groundwater monitoring wells (MW01-MW06) and targeted test pits. Samples were analysed for total recoverable hydrocarbons (TRH), BTEXN, PAHs and metals by Southern Cross Analytical (fictional laboratory). QA/QC included field duplicates (RPD within 30%) and laboratory blanks, all acceptable.

## 3 Results

Two areas exceed the adopted criteria.

| Location | Analyte | Result | Criterion | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TP14 (fill, 0.3 m) | Lead | 2,300 mg/kg | 1,500 mg/kg | NEPM ASC Sch B1 HIL D |
| BH07 (0.5 m) | TRH F2 | 410 mg/kg | adopted HSL (see RAP) | Health screening level, commercial/industrial |

Groundwater at MW01-MW06 did not exceed the adopted groundwater criteria.

## 4 Conclusions and recommendations

Lead in fill at TP14 and hydrocarbons at BH07 exceed the adopted criteria and require remediation or management. A remedial action plan (RAP) should evaluate excavation and off-site disposal against ex-situ treatment. Groundwater should be re-checked after any excavation.
