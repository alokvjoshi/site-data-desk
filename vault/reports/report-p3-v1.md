# Asbestos Assessment and Remediation Plan - Brightwater Estate Stage 3 (DRAFT v1)

**Version:** v0.9 (Draft)

## 1 Introduction

This draft report presents the asbestos assessment of Brightwater Estate Stage 3, a residential subdivision for Brightwater Homes (fictional). The site end use is residential with garden access, so the residential A screening levels in NEPM ASC Schedule B1 apply.

## 2 Results

Sixty test pits were excavated on a 25 m grid. Bonded ACM was found in 9 of 60 test pits, all in imported fill. The maximum bonded ACM was 0.03% w/w at TP22, exceeding the residential A screening level of 0.01% w/w. Fibrous asbestos and asbestos fines were below the 0.001% w/w screening level (maximum 0.0008% w/w at TP22).

## 3 Remediation approach

ACM-impacted fill will be removed off-site by a licensed removalist. The estimated volume of fill to be removed is 1,200 m3, based on the test pit logs. Clearance will be by visual inspection and validation sampling by a licensed asbestos assessor.

## 4 Programme

Removal is planned to begin in June 2026 and to be completed in mid-July 2026, with handover to the builder in August 2026.
