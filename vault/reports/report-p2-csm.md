# Conceptual Site Model Update - Marlin Bay Airport

**Version:** v1.0 (Final)  
**Date:** 2026-08-04  
**Author:** [[Marcus Oyelaran]]  
**Project:** [[p2-marlin-bay-pfas]]

> FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT. Not a real report.

## 1 Source

The source is the former fire training pad, where aqueous film-forming foam was historically used. Soil impact is concentrated in the top 1 m under the pad.

## 2 Pathways and receptors

Infiltration to shallow groundwater, then lateral migration to the east. Off-site surface water is the key receptor and was non-detect for PFOS and PFHxS at all six off-site points in Round 2. There is no currently complete exposure pathway to people. Future groundwater use is a potential pathway.

## 3 Trend

At MW04, PFOS + PFHxS decreased from 12.4 ug/L (Round 1) to 9.8 ug/L (Round 2), a decrease of about 21%. The concentration is still far above the 0.07 ug/L screening value.

## 4 Uncertainties

The extent of the plume east of MW13 is not yet delineated. The regulator may require additional off-site delineation.
