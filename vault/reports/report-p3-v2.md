# Asbestos Assessment, Removal and Clearance Report - Brightwater Estate Stage 3 (FINAL v2)

**Version:** v2.0 (Final)

## 1 Introduction

This final report presents the asbestos assessment, removal and clearance of Brightwater Estate Stage 3, a residential subdivision for Brightwater Homes (fictional). The site end use is residential with garden access, so the residential A screening levels in NEPM ASC Schedule B1 apply.

## 2 Results

Sixty test pits were excavated on a 25 m grid. Bonded ACM was found in 9 of 60 test pits, all in imported fill. The maximum bonded ACM was 0.03% w/w at TP22, exceeding the residential A screening level of 0.01% w/w. Fibrous asbestos and asbestos fines were below the 0.001% w/w screening level (maximum 0.0008% w/w at TP22). Benzo(a)pyrene TEQ at TP12 was 4.6 mg/kg against the residential A HIL of 3 mg/kg.

## 3 Remediation and volume

ACM-impacted fill was removed off-site by Redgum Remediation, a licensed removalist (fictional). The final surveyed volume removed was 1,150 m3. This supersedes the 1,200 m3 estimate in the draft report. An additional ACM pocket near TP31 was found and removed.

## 4 Clearance

A clearance inspection on 24 July 2026 found no visible ACM and validation samples were below screening levels. The clearance certificate was issued on 3 August 2026.
