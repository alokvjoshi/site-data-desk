## 1 Summary

Round 4 monitoring was completed at six wells. Groundwater pH at MW02 has improved to 5.6 but remains below the trigger range of 6.5 to 8.5. Dissolved aluminium at MW05 is elevated.

## 2 Results

| Well | pH | Dissolved zinc (mg/L) | Dissolved aluminium (mg/L) |
|---|---|---|---|
| MW02 | 5.6 | 0.09 | 0.4 |
| MW05 | 6.9 | 0.03 | 1.8 |
| MW06 | 7.4 | 0.02 | 0.2 |

Trigger values: pH 6.5 to 8.5; zinc 0.08 mg/L; aluminium 0.5 mg/L.

## 3 Interpretation

The aluminium exceedance at MW05 is not explained by the treatment pond discharge. A verification resample confirmed the result (1.7 mg/L). The trend at MW02 suggests slow recovery after the earlier discharge pH excursion on 4 May 2026.

## 4 Recommendations

Continue quarterly monitoring and consider extending to ten rounds to confirm the trend. A contingency plan may be required if aluminium persists.
