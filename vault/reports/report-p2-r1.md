## 1 Scope and methods

Round 1 sampling covered 40 soil samples, 12 groundwater wells and 8 surface water points under the agreed sampling and analysis quality plan (SAQP). PFAS-free protocols were used. Field blanks and rinsate blanks were collected every 20 samples.

## 2 Screening values

Results are compared with project-adopted screening values for the purposes of this fictional demonstration: 0.07 ug/L for PFOS + PFHxS and 0.56 ug/L for PFOA in groundwater (drinking water basis). These values are illustrative and are not restated from any standard in this knowledge base.

The highest soil concentrations are in the top 1 m beneath the former training pad.

## 4 QA/QC and next steps

Surrogate recoveries were 82-110% and field blanks were below the limit of reporting. A second round and off-site surface water sampling to the east are recommended.
